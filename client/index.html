Rules of Twelve Janggi

Pieces & Movement

  • King (王): 1 step in all 8 directions.
  • General (將): 1 step orthogonally (Forward, Back, Left, Right).
  • Minister (相): 1 step diagonally.
  • Man (兵): 1 step forward only.
  • Feudal Lord (侯): Promoted Man inside enemy territory. Moves everywhere except diagonally backward.

Captives & Drops

Captured pieces enter your reserve. On your turn, drop a captive into any empty tile outside opponent territory.

Winning Conditions

  • Capture the opponent's King.
  • Enter opponent's territory with King and survive 1 full turn.
